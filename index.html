CMBS Pricer

Commercial mortgage-backed securities deal analyser. Automates all processes from loan tape to tranche price.

Projects quarterly cash flows for a pool of commercial real estate loans, runs them through a note waterfall and prices each class off a rating and WAL spread curve. Outputs price, yield, discount margin and duration per tranche, models call protection, and stresses losses across value declines and default rates.
